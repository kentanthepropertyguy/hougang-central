The case

What works. What to weigh.

StrengthsWhy buyers are looking

  • Everything at the doorstep.MRT, bus interchange and a planned new mall on the same site.
  • A future interchange station.The Cross Island Line is planned to meet the North East Line at Hougang.
  • Little new supply nearby.The first government land sale in Hougang since 2019.
  • Two established developers.CapitaLand Development and UOL share the residential joint venture.

ConsiderationsBefore you commit

  • A clear premium over resale.Early estimates sit roughly 30–55% above recent prices at the nearest 2018-lease condos. Compare at the same total budget, not just psf.
  • A busy address.Mall and interchange footfall means more activity around the homes than in a purely residential condo.
  • A long build.The mall is targeted for 2030–2031 and no completion date has been given for the homes yet. Expect works at the town centre for several years.
  • The details that decide value are unknown.Unit mix, stack facing and maintenance fees will matter, and none are published yet.

Who it may suit

A good fit for some buyers. Not all.

Likely fit

Hougang upgraders who want to stay

HDB owners who know the town and want to move up without moving away from family and schools.

Likely fit

Households who rely on public transport

Daily MRT and bus users who want that to stay easy as the network grows.

Think carefully

Buyers after quiet or the lowest psf

Nearby resale condos may offer more space for the money, without the footfall of a town hub.
